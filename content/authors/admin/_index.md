I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Engineering at Universidad de los Andes, Colombia, specializing in multi-agent AI systems and cooperative resilience. My research investigates how groups of artificial agents—and, more recently, humans and LLM-based agents—maintain collective welfare under disruptions. I study how incentives, reward learning, communication, and local interaction rules can support sustained cooperation in mixed-motive environments. I began my Ph.D. in 2023 with support from the competitive Google DeepMind–CinfonIA Scholarship. My interdisciplinary background includes an M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics and a B.Sc. in Electronic Engineering, which provide the mathematical, computational, and systems foundations for my current research.
